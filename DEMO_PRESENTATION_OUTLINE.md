## Presentation Tips

### Before Presenting:
1. **Test everything** - Run through the demo 3x
2. **Have a backup** - Record a video of the demo in case of issues
3. **Know your audience** - Skip/add slides based on who's in the room
4. **Time yourself** - Rehearse to stay under 7 minutes

### During Presentation:
1. **Start strong** - Hook them with the problem (Slide 2)
2. **Keep momentum** - Don't linger on any slide too long
3. **Make it live** - Do the demo live if possible, builds credibility
4. **Engage the audience** - Ask "Who spends time on this?" before Slide 2
5. **Handle questions** - "Great question, let me address that at the end"

### After Presenting:
1. **Capture interest** - Have a signup sheet or Slack channel ready
2. **Follow up** - Send slides + demo recording to attendees
3. **Measure impact** - Track who signs up for early access

## Risk Mitigation

### What if...

**Demo fails completely?**
- Switch to pre-recorded video
- "Let me show you what this looks like"
- Don't apologize excessively, move on

**MCP server is slow?**
- Narrate while waiting: "Querying our production database..."
- Have a joke ready: "Even when it's thinking, it's faster than manual!"

**Wrong data returns?**
- Adapt: "Interesting, let me refine that query..."
- Show how Claude can iterate

**Someone asks a question you can't answer?**
- "Great question - let me connect you with [expert] after this"
- Write it down visibly so they know you captured it
